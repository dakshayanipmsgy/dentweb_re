Financing questions, answered.

Can I prepay the loan?

Ask your lender for the current prepayment and foreclosure terms for your specific product. Do not rely on a general rule about charges or waiting periods.

Is insurance included?

Confirm whether insurance is included, optional, or required, and review its cost, exclusions, and coverage with the provider.

When can installation begin?

The schedule depends on the agreed project scope, approvals, finance availability, materials, and site readiness. Our team will confirm a plan for your project.
